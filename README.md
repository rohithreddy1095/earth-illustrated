# Earth, Illustrated

An illustrated, interactive reference for building with earth, straw, lime and bamboo: soil tests, walls, roofs, lime, sun and heat, twenty earth houses from India and around the world, and a library of every source it draws on.

**Open it:** https://rohithreddy1095.github.io/earth-illustrated/

Set your house once at the top (town, size, rooms, walls, roof, soil and the people who will live in it) and the stage-by-stage guide, the calendar against the monsoon, the costing and the figures follow it. The settings and your ticks stay in your browser. It covers the services as well: the kitchen and hearth, the bathroom, plumbing, and wiring, earthing and rooftop solar.

Every number sits in the material data table with its source and a confidence rating, and the Library section lists every document behind the book, with the sections that use it. The content is awaiting review by practising builders: treat it as a guide, test your own soil, and consult a local engineer and an experienced builder for anything you will live in.

The whole site is one HTML file (`index.html`); the 3D models use three.js r128 from cdnjs.

The animations are drawn for this book from the sources cited beside each one; nothing is loaded from video sites.
